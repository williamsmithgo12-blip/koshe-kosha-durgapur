The Koshe Kosha Experience

Where Bengal's culinary heritage meets contemporary hospitality.

For over a decade, Koshe Kosha has worked to bring back the authenticity of Bengal's culinary heritage — presenting it in a way that feels warm, current and deeply familiar. Every slow-cooked kosha, every hand-ground spice and every plate that leaves our kitchen carries a story from Bengal's kitchens.

Born from a vision to take the flavours that form the unique identity of Bengali cuisine to a wider audience, Koshe Kosha offers a distinctly Bangaliana experience — rooted in tradition, served with quiet elegance.

Our Story

Rooted in Bengal.

For over a decade, Koshe Kosha has worked to preserve the flavours, traditions and character of Bengal while presenting them in a contemporary dining environment. What began as a commitment to authenticity became a place where families gather, stories are shared and recipes are honoured.

From the slow, patient kosha technique to the mustard-oil finish on our fish, nothing here is rushed. Because the food of Bengal was never meant to be.

Heritage Recipes drawn from Bengal's regional kitchens and family tables.
Craft Hand-ground spices, slow cooking and techniques that take time.
Hospitality The warmth of a Bengali home, brought into a contemporary dining room.
Experience A meal that feels familiar, and still feels like an occasion.
